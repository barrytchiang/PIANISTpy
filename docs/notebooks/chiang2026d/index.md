# Chiang+(2026d) figures

Source: https://pianistpy.com/docs/notebooks/chiang2026d/

Each notebook reproduces one or more result figures of [Chiang+(2026d)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28758) with the released package, and computes every number
it shows in its own cells.

- [Distribution functions of the NFW families](https://pianistpy.com/docs/notebooks/chiang2026d/fig04_05_06)

- [Anisotropy of superposed models and of two fits](https://pianistpy.com/docs/notebooks/chiang2026d/fig07)

- [Benchmark of the isotropic NFW distribution function](https://pianistpy.com/docs/notebooks/chiang2026d/fig08)

- [Local and line-of-sight velocity distributions](https://pianistpy.com/docs/notebooks/chiang2026d/fig13_15_16)

- [Accuracy checks of the distribution function and the velocity distribution](https://pianistpy.com/docs/notebooks/chiang2026d/figC1_C2)
