# Chiang+(2026d) figures

Source: https://pianistpy.com/docs/notebooks/chiang2026d/

it shows in its own cells.

- [Fig. 8: Isotropic NFW benchmark against other codes](https://pianistpy.com/docs/notebooks/chiang2026d/fig08)

- [Figs. 13, 15 and 16: Velocity distributions and observables](https://pianistpy.com/docs/notebooks/chiang2026d/fig13_15_16)
