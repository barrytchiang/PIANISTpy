*N*-body tests with GAMER
=========================

The test problem of the isolated *N*-body runs of |paper|, `Chiang et al. (2025)
<https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/staf1639>`__ and `Chiang et al. (2026a) <https://doi.org/10.33232/001c.155367>`__,
as a test problem of the `GAMER <https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/sty2586>`__ code with its analysis and plotting
scripts, is listed on the :doc:`Upcoming features <../project/upcoming-features>` page.
