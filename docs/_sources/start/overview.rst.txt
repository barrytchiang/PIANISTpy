What PIANISTpy does
===================

.. The landing page shows this list as written here: docs/tools/assemble_site.py copies it from the built page,
   links and all. Edit the list here only.

.. rst-class:: features

- :doc:`Density profiles. <../guide/profiles>` Any double power law in its three slopes, with the
  Navarro-Frenk-White, Hernquist and Plummer profiles as named presets.
- :doc:`Velocity anisotropy. <../guide/anisotropy>` Isotropic, constant, Osipkov-Merritt, and the generalized
  Osipkov-Merritt (Cuddeford) models, radially biased (Type I) or tangentially biased (Type II).
- :doc:`Distribution functions. <../guide/df>` The distribution function of each model, from the analytic
  inversion of its density, returned whether or not it is physical, with a statement of where it is not.
- :doc:`Truncation in phase space. <../guide/truncation>` Method I truncates a model's own distribution function
  at a user-specified energy, or at the energy that yields a target mass or a target radius. Method II gives a new
  anisotropy to a density that is already truncated.
- :doc:`Particles. <../guide/particles>` Positions and velocities drawn by inversion, with no rejection step, and
  reproducible particle by particle.
- :doc:`Observables. <../guide/observables>` Intrinsic and line-of-sight velocity dispersions, line-of-sight
  velocity distributions, and their averages over apertures.
- :ref:`Superposition. <guide-superposition>` Distribution functions inverted from one truncated density,
  combined with positive weights that sum to one into one equilibrium model.
- :ref:`Inference. <guide-inference>` The log-likelihood of a model against data, as a JAX function of the
  components' weights and the scales of the units.
