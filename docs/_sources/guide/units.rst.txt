Units
=====

Everything is computed and returned in internal units, the dimensionless normalization of Widrow (2000) in which
the scale radius and the central potential of the untruncated profile are 1. A result's ``to`` method presents it in
model units (:math:`G = M_0 = r_0 = 1`) or, through `Astropy <https://www.astropy.org/>`_, in physical units,
without recomputing it, and what is computed from it is returned in that system.

Three inputs of a truncation are given in the units that are natural for them, as their documentation states:
the truncation energy ``k_t`` in internal units, a target radius ``r_t`` in units of the scale radius, and a
target mass ``mass_t`` in units of :math:`M_0`, together with the concentration that defines :math:`M_0`.
