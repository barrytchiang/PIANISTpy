Units#

Module pianistpy.units

Unit systems of results and conversion factors out of internal units.

A result presents the result’s quantities in another unit system through the result’s to method, which builds a Units object. unit_factors returns the conversion factors of a density profile as a UnitFactors object.

class pianistpy.units.UnitFactors(rho_norm, mass_q, model_v, model_mass, model_rho, model_p, physical_r_pc=None, physical_v_kms=None, physical_mass_msun=None, physical_rho_msun_pc3=None, physical_p_kms2=None, physical_rho_0_msun_pc3=None)#

Factors that convert quantities from internal units to model and physical units.

A quantity in internal units is multiplied by the factor of the quantity’s dimension to obtain the quantity in the target units. The model units have \(G = M_0 = r_0 = 1\), with \(M_0\) the mass inside the radius \(c\,r_0\) and \(c\) the concentration given to unit_factors. A length has no model factor, because internal units and model units both measure a length in units of \(r_0\).

Parameters:

  • rho_norm (float) – Normalization \(C\) of the density profile (DoublePowerLaw.rho_norm).

  • mass_q (float) – The number \(q = \mathcal{M}(c)/C\): the mass inside the radius \(c\,r_0\) in units of \(4\pi\rho_0 r_0^3\).

  • model_v (float) – Factor that converts a velocity to model units, \((C q)^{-1/2}\).

  • model_mass (float) – Factor that converts a mass to model units, \((C q)^{-1}\).

  • model_rho (float) – Factor that converts a density to model units, \((4\pi C q)^{-1}\).

  • model_p (float) – Factor that converts a potential to model units, \((C q)^{-1}\).

  • physical_r_pc (float or None, optional) – Factor that converts a length to parsecs, equal to \(r_0\) in parsecs. Default: None, which means unit_factors received no physical scales.

  • physical_v_kms (float or None, optional) – Factor that converts a velocity to kilometers per second. Default: None, which means unit_factors received no physical scales.

  • physical_mass_msun (float or None, optional) – Factor that converts a mass to solar masses. Default: None, which means unit_factors received no physical scales.

  • physical_rho_msun_pc3 (float or None, optional) – Factor that converts a density to solar masses per cubic parsec. Default: None, which means unit_factors received no physical scales.

  • physical_p_kms2 (float or None, optional) – Factor that converts a potential to the square of kilometers per second. Default: None, which means unit_factors received no physical scales.

  • physical_rho_0_msun_pc3 (float or None, optional) – The scale density \(\rho_0\) in solar masses per cubic parsec. Default: None, which means unit_factors received no physical scales.

See also

unit_factors

The function that builds the factors of a density profile.

Units

The unit system a result carries.

Notes

Every physical factor is None unless unit_factors received both physical scales, \(M_0\) in solar masses and \(r_0\) in parsecs.

Examples

>>> from pianistpy import DoublePowerLaw
>>> from pianistpy.units import unit_factors
>>> factors = unit_factors(DoublePowerLaw.nfw(), concentration=10.0)
>>> round(factors.model_mass, 6)
0.67168
>>> factors.physical_r_pc is None
True
class pianistpy.units.Units(system='internal', concentration=None, mass_0_msun=None, r_0_pc=None)#

The unit system of a result and the scales that define the unit system.

A result holds the result’s numbers in internal units and hands the numbers over in the unit system that the result’s Units object sets. Units.build builds a Units object from scales in any unit; the constructor takes the scales in solar masses and parsecs.

Parameters:

  • system ({"internal", "model", "physical"}, optional) – The unit system. "internal": \(G = \rho_0/C = r_0 = 1\), with \(\rho_0\) the scale density and \(C\) the normalization of the density profile. "model": \(G = M_0 = r_0 = 1\), with \(M_0\) the mass inside the radius \(c\,r_0\). "physical": Astropy quantities in parsecs, kilometers per second, solar masses, solar masses per cubic parsec and \({\rm km^2\,s^{-2}}\). Default: "internal", which means the result hands the result’s quantities over in internal units.

  • concentration (float, optional) – The concentration \(c\), which defines \(M_0\) as the mass inside the radius \(c\,r_0\). Required by the model units and the physical units. Default: None, which means no concentration, as the internal units require.

  • mass_0_msun (float, optional) – The mass \(M_0\) in solar masses. Required by the physical units. Default: None, which means no physical mass scale.

  • r_0_pc (float, optional) – The scale radius \(r_0\) in parsecs. Required by the physical units. Default: None, which means no physical length scale.

Raises:

  • InputError – If system is not "internal", "model" or "physical".

  • InputError – If system is "model" or "physical" and concentration is None.

  • InputError – If system is "physical" and mass_0_msun or r_0_pc is None.

See also

Units.build

classmethod build(system, concentration=None, mass_0=None, r_0=None, r_vir=None)#

Return the units of a unit system from user-specified scales in any unit.

The internal units ignore every scale. The model units keep the concentration and ignore the physical scales. The physical units keep the concentration, \(M_0\) in solar masses and \(r_0\) in parsecs.

Parameters:

  • system ({"internal", "model", "physical"}) – The unit system of the returned units.

  • concentration (float, optional) – The concentration \(c\), which defines \(M_0\) as the mass inside the radius \(c\,r_0\). Positive. Required by the model units and the physical units. Default: None, which means no concentration.

  • mass_0 (float or astropy Quantity, optional) – The mass \(M_0\). A float is in solar masses; a quantity of any mass unit is converted to solar masses. Positive. Required by the physical units. Default: None, which means no mass scale.

  • r_0 (float or astropy Quantity, optional) – The scale radius \(r_0\). A float is in parsecs; a quantity of any length unit is converted to parsecs. Positive. Default: None, which means the scale radius is taken from r_vir.

  • r_vir (float or astropy Quantity, optional) – The radius \(c\,r_0\), given in place of r_0. A float is in parsecs; a quantity of any length unit is converted to parsecs. Positive. Default: None, which means the scale radius is taken from r_0.

Returns:

units – The units of system, with \(M_0\) in solar masses and \(r_0\) in parsecs.

Return type:

Units

Raises:

  • InputError – If both r_0 and r_vir are given.

  • InputError – If concentration, r_0, r_vir or, for the physical units, mass_0 is given and is not positive.

  • InputError – If r_vir is given and concentration is not.

  • InputError – If system is not "internal", "model" or "physical".

  • InputError – If system is "model" or "physical" and concentration is None.

  • InputError – If system is "physical" and mass_0 is None, or neither r_0 nor r_vir is given.

See also

Model.to

The model presented in the unit system that a Units object sets.

Examples

>>> from pianistpy.units import Units
>>> Units.build("internal", concentration=10.0)
Units(system='internal', concentration=None, mass_0_msun=None, r_0_pc=None)
>>> Units.build("physical", concentration=10.0, mass_0=1e12, r_0=2e4).r_0_pc
20000.0

convert(kind, value, profile)#

Return a quantity given in internal units in the unit system of the units.

Parameters:

  • kind ({"length", "velocity", "mass", "density", "potential"}) – The physical dimension of the quantity.

  • value (array-like) – The quantity, in internal units.

  • profile (DoublePowerLaw) – The density profile, which fixes the normalization \(C\) of the density.

Returns:

converted – The quantity, with the shape of value. A float64 array in the internal units and in the model units; an Astropy quantity in the physical units. A length has the same value in internal units and in model units, because both unit systems measure a length in units of \(r_0\).

Return type:

array or astropy Quantity

Raises:

  • InputError – If kind is not "length", "velocity", "mass", "density" or "potential".

  • InputError – If the unit system is not the internal one and factors raises InputError for the units’ scales.

See also

Units.factors

The conversion factors the conversion multiplies by.

Examples

>>> from pianistpy import DoublePowerLaw
>>> from pianistpy.units import Units
>>> units = Units.build("model", concentration=10.0)
>>> units.convert("length", [1.0, 2.0], DoublePowerLaw.nfw())
array([1., 2.])
>>> round(float(units.convert("mass", 1.0, DoublePowerLaw.nfw())), 6)

pianistpy.units.unit_factors(profile, concentration, mass_0_msun=None, r_0_pc=None)#

Return the factors that convert a profile’s quantities out of internal units.

The model-unit factors depend on the density profile and the concentration \(c\) alone. The physical factors also need the two physical scales, \(M_0\) in solar masses and \(r_0\) in parsecs.

Parameters:

  • profile (DoublePowerLaw) – The density profile. The profile fixes the normalization \(C\) and the mass \(\mathcal{M}(c)\) inside the radius \(c\,r_0\).

  • concentration (float) – The concentration \(c\): the radius, in units of the scale radius \(r_0\), inside which the mass \(M_0\) is defined. Positive.

  • mass_0_msun (float, optional) – The mass \(M_0\) in solar masses. Positive. Given together with r_0_pc or not at all. Default: None, which means only the model-unit factors are formed.

  • r_0_pc (float, optional) – The scale radius \(r_0\) in parsecs. Positive. Given together with mass_0_msun or not at all. Default: None, which means only the model-unit factors are formed.

Returns:

factors – The model-unit factors, and the physical factors when both mass_0_msun and r_0_pc are given. Every physical factor is None when neither mass_0_msun nor r_0_pc is given.

Return type:

UnitFactors

Raises:

  • InputError – If concentration is not positive.

  • InputError – If one of mass_0_msun and r_0_pc is given and the other is not.

See also

UnitFactors

The factors and their definitions.

Units.factors

The factors for the scales a result carries.

Notes

With \(\rho_{\rm w} = \rho_0/C\), the internal unit of mass is \(M_{\rm W} = 4\pi\rho_{\rm w} r_0^3\), the internal unit of velocity is \(v_{\rm W} = (4\pi G\rho_{\rm w} r_0^2)^{1/2}\), and the mass inside the radius \(c\,r_0\) is \(M_0 = 4\pi\rho_0 r_0^3\,q\). A velocity in internal units is therefore multiplied by \((C q)^{-1/2}\) to reach model units, a mass and a potential by \((C q)^{-1}\), and a density by \((4\pi C q)^{-1}\).

References

Chiang+(2026d), Section 2.1 and Eq. (6).

Examples

>>> import math
>>> from pianistpy import DoublePowerLaw
>>> factors = unit_factors(DoublePowerLaw.nfw(), concentration=10.0)
>>> factors.rho_norm
1.0
>>> round(factors.mass_q, 12) == round(math.log(11.0) - 10.0 / 11.0, 12)
True
>>> physical = unit_factors(
...     DoublePowerLaw.nfw(), 10.0, mass_0_msun=1e12, r_0_pc=2e4
... )
>>> physical.physical_r_pc
20000.0
