Units (pianistpy.units)#

Conversion from internal units to model and physical units.

class pianistpy.units.UnitFactors(rho_norm, mass_q, model_v, model_mass, model_rho, model_p, physical_r_pc=None, physical_v_kms=None, physical_mass_msun=None, physical_rho_msun_pc3=None, physical_p_kms2=None, physical_rho_0_msun_pc3=None)#

Factors that convert internal units to model and to physical units.

A quantity in internal units is multiplied by the factor to obtain it in the target units. The model units have \(G = M_0 = r_0 = 1\), with \(M_0\) the mass inside the radius concentration times \(r_0\).

Parameters:

  • rho_norm (float) – Normalisation \(C\) of the density profile.

  • mass_q (float) – \(q = \mathcal{M}(c)/C\), the mass inside the concentration radius in units of \(4\pi\rho_0 r_0^3\).

  • model_v (float) – Factors to the model units, for a speed, a mass, a density and a potential.

  • model_mass (float) – Factors to the model units, for a speed, a mass, a density and a potential.

  • model_rho (float) – Factors to the model units, for a speed, a mass, a density and a potential.

  • model_p (float) – Factors to the model units, for a speed, a mass, a density and a potential.

  • physical_r_pc (float or None) – Factors to parsecs, kilometres per second and solar masses.

  • physical_v_kms (float or None) – Factors to parsecs, kilometres per second and solar masses.

  • physical_mass_msun (float or None) – Factors to parsecs, kilometres per second and solar masses.

  • physical_rho_msun_pc3 (float or None) – Factors to solar masses per cubic parsec, and to the square of kilometres per second.

  • physical_p_kms2 (float or None) – Factors to solar masses per cubic parsec, and to the square of kilometres per second.

  • physical_rho_0_msun_pc3 (float or None) – The scale density \(\rho_0\) in solar masses per cubic parsec.

Notes

The physical factors are None unless both physical scales were given to unit_factors.

class pianistpy.units.Units(system='internal', concentration=None, mass_0_msun=None, r_0_pc=None)#

The unit system of a result and the scales that define it.

Parameters:

  • system ({"internal", "model", "physical"}) – "internal": \(G = \rho_0/C = r_0 = 1\). "model": \(G = M_0 = r_0 = 1\). "physical": Astropy quantities.

  • concentration (float, optional) – Concentration \(c\) that defines \(M_0\) as the mass inside \(c\,r_0\). Needed by the model and the physical systems.

  • mass_0_msun (float, optional) – \(M_0\) in solar masses. Needed by the physical system.

  • r_0_pc (float, optional) – Scale radius \(r_0\) in parsecs. Needed by the physical system.

Examples

>>> Units().system
'internal'
>>> Units.build("model", concentration=10.0).concentration
10.0

pianistpy.units.unit_factors(profile, concentration, mass_0_msun=None, r_0_pc=None)#

Return the conversion factors of a profile.

Parameters:

  • profile (DoublePowerLaw) – The density profile, which fixes the normalization \(C\).

  • concentration (float) – Radius, in units of the scale radius \(r_0\), inside which the mass \(M_0\) is defined. Positive.

  • mass_0_msun (float, optional) – \(M_0\) in solar masses.

  • r_0_pc (float, optional) – Scale radius \(r_0\) in parsecs.

Returns:

factors – The model-unit factors, and the physical factors if both mass_0_msun and r_0_pc are given.

Return type:

UnitFactors

Notes

With \(\rho_{\rm w} = \rho_0/C\) the internal units are \(M_{\rm W} = 4\pi\rho_{\rm w} r_0^3\) for mass and \(v_{\rm W} = \sqrt{4\pi G\rho_{\rm w} r_0^2}\) for speed, and \(M_0 = 4\pi\rho_0 r_0^3\,q\). Hence a speed is multiplied by \((C q)^{-1/2}\), a mass and a potential by \((C q)^{-1}\), and a density by \((4\pi C q)^{-1}\) to reach the model units.

References

Paper I, Section on units (labels ssec:Units, eqn:M_nor_1).

Examples

>>> factors = unit_factors(DoublePowerLaw.nfw(), concentration=10.0)
>>> factors.rho_norm
1.0
>>> round(factors.mass_q, 12) == round(math.log(11.0) - 10.0 / 11.0, 12)
True
